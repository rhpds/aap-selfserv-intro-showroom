= Workshop details

== What's in your lab

Your environment is pre-deployed for this workshop with the following components:

* **Ansible Automation Platform 2.7**: Pre-configured with users, inventories, credentials, projects, and job templates
* **Automation Portal**: Deployed and connected to that AAP instance for catalog, content discovery, and EE Builder
* **Private Automation Hub**: Hosts collections used by discovery and EE builds
* **GitHub organization** (link:https://github.com/AAP-Automation-Portal-Lab[AAP-Automation-Portal-Lab^]): Pre-created EE repositories for Module 5
* **OpenShift**: hosts AAP and the portal (console available for reference and not actively used in exercises)

== Browser best practices

[IMPORTANT]
====
When switching users between AAP and Automation Portal:

* Prefer **new-tab** links to the portal (GitHub OAuth in Module 5 cannot run reliably inside the Showroom iframe)
* Between personas, **log out of both Ansible Automation Platform and Automation Portal** before signing in as the next user.
====
